## Why this addendum exists

The Master Brand System nailed **how brands look and how content ships** (palettes, Identity Engine, Sticker Book, shared engines, workflows).

After that, the product front door clarified as **two buttons**, and a forge-metaphor vocabulary was recovered. Some older names can stay in the back room on purpose. This page connects the layers without forcing every forge word onto the home screen.

---

## Home screen (what humans see)

| Button | Door | Feels like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This is You** | A | Identity, voice, look, posts, ideas, “who I am” |
| **Your World** | B | Business, money, leads, campaigns, ops, “everything I’m running” |

One app. Two doors. Same account / workspace spine.

---

## How Master Brand System pieces map

| Master Brand System (June) | Natural home |
| --- | --- |
| Identity Engine, Sticker Book, Template Gallery, first-session brand kit | **This is You** (and shared under the hood) |
| Social templates, Photo Transformation, Palette Enforcement, Export Router | Shared — used heavily from **This is You**; available in **Your World** |
| Brand Vault (asset locker) | Shared storage (not “money Vault”) |
| Creator Marketplace | Bridges both (create in A, commerce/ops toward B) |
| T&T Community Feed / Story Engine | Often entered via **This is You** for the artist brand; community ops may surface in **Your World** |
| King’s Corner / CEO desk capabilities (money, leads, pipelines, song charts) | **Your World** — not fully spelled in Master Brand System; added by DR-001 |

## Forge vocabulary (optional depth — not required on the two buttons)

| Name | Role | Gravity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sword | Branding, identity, creative, discovery | This is You |
| Foundry | Creation / transformation | This is You + shared engines |
| Anvil | Build, execute, launch, publish | Both (publish from A; projects from B) |
| Whetstone | Refine / optimize | Both |
| Scabbard | Organize / integrate | Both |
| Citadel | Business systems / ops hub | Your World |
| Shield | Security / legal | Your World (platform-wide) |
| Vault | Financial / protected value | Your World — **≠** Brand Vault assets |

Logo hint in Master Brand System (anvil / forge-hammer) already agrees with this family.

---

## What we intentionally do *not* put on the two buttons

- Sword / Citadel / full forge set
- “Quick Post” / “Quick Thought”
- Long line “Your dreams coming true” (spirit lives in **Your World**)

Lost-for-good-reason: home screen stays scannable in two seconds.

---

## Build Lab note

Implement home CTAs as **This is You** | **Your World**. Keep Master Brand System palettes and engine names in code/docs. Add forge labels in nav/IA only when they help — never block shipping the two doors.
