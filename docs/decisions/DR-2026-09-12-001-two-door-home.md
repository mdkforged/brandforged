## Decision

Brand Forged ships as **one application** with a **two-door home screen**:

1. **Door A — Quick Post / Quick Thought** (final label TBD)  
   Fast path for brand & content: one-button multi-social publish, music/song ideas, brand think-tank, notes/safe vault, AI brainstorm companion, reminders. Anything business-related captured here **must also persist on Door B**.

2. **Door B — Your dreams coming true**  
   Full business OS desk (King’s Corner lineage): money in/out, leads, campaigns, SEO/analytics, per-song charts (Spotify / Apple / Amazon / YouTube — owned analytics), pipelines, reminders. Shared memory with Door A.

**Packaging:** clients may take **both doors** (full out-of-box) or **Door A only** (content/brand engine).

**Motto retained:** from the average Joe / first-time creator to the CEO — one button that does everything. “Average Joe” is a **job**, not a fame level; major artists (including Tethered & Truth by MDK) use Door A and may use Door B to run the artist business.

## Why (grounds in Canon)

This decision **implements**, does not replace:

| Canon source | How this decision aligns |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform Doctrine I — One Platform** | “Brand Forged is one platform. Applications are expressions of the platform.” Two doors = two expressions, one app/account/workspace spine. |
| **Doctrine II — One Identity** | Same environment for creating, publishing, managing, and operating. |
| **Doctrine V — One AI** | Shared AI brain across doors; specialists, one coordinated intelligence. |
| **Doctrine VI — One Workspace** | Projects belong to the user; doors contribute capabilities. |
| **Doctrine VIII — One Source of Truth** | Notes/reminders/business facts have one store; Door A writes through to Door B. |
| **Doctrine IX — One Experience** | Familiar navigation and AI behavior across both desks. |
| **Doctrine X — One Purpose** | “How does this help someone build something meaningful?” |
| **Strategy Room Order No. 001** | Cease endless foundation authoring; Build Lab implements product. |
| **Institutional Boundaries** | Simplicity Before Complexity; Reuse Before Reinvention; One platform not two companies. |
