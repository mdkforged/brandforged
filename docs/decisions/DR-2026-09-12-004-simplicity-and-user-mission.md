## Decision

Brand Forged stays **simple for the user**.

1. **Public language** uses plain human words (e.g. home: **This is You** / **Your World**). Forge-era names (Sword, Citadel, Shield, Anvil, Foundry, Whetstone, Vault, Scabbard, etc.) are **internal only** — founder/context map, not customer-facing chrome. Most users would never know what those meant; they should not have to.

2. **Mission filter** for every feature, flow, and AI behavior:  
   - Does this **help** and **work for** the user?  
   - Does it **give them time back**?  
   - Is it **only a positive** in their life?  
   If no → don’t ship it (or cut it).

3. **Never should the user work for AI.** The AI (and the app) work for the user — not the reverse. No busywork to “feed the model,” no ritual prompts required to get basic value, no making people serve the system.

4. Stay true to the founder’s self, to the original spirit of AI as a helper when it entered the scene, and to that mission — not to clever naming or complexity for its own sake.
