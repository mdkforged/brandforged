## Decision

Brand Forged home screen shows **exactly two buttons**:

1. **This is You** — Door A (content / brand / identity / quick create & publish path)
2. **Your World** — Door B (business / ops / CEO desk / King’s Corner lineage)

Plain human labels on the glass. Forge names (Sword, Citadel, …) and older working titles may live one level deeper or in docs — they do not have to be the two big buttons.
